# 0002 · MIT licence, no brand logos

**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Context
The kit names devices made by other companies. Logos are trademarks; their use implies endorsement.

## Decision
MIT licence, copyright "Dr4w". No brand logos anywhere in the kit or the showcase: generic device
icons (TV, console, controller, lamp) and plain text such as "Works with Android TV" or
"Works with Nintendo Switch".

## Consequences
Icons are drawn in the repo (`stroke="currentColor"`, one stroke weight). Product names appear only
as text describing compatibility.

## Status
Accepted.
